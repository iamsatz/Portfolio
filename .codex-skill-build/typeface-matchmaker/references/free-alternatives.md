# Free Typeface Alternatives

Use this as a substitution map. It is organized by the role a paid font usually plays. Verify current licensing for commercial work, especially for fonts from Behance, personal sites, or GitHub.

## Contemporary Grotesk / Neo-Grotesk

Use when the target resembles: Suisse, Aeonik, Roobert, Lab Grotesque, Sharp Sans, Haffer, TT Neoris, TT Hoves, Scandium, Post Grotesk, CoFo Sans, General Grotesque, Neue Rational.

- Inter: safest UI default; neutral, dense ecosystem, excellent for product.
- Geist: cleaner and more current than Inter; good for modern SaaS and dashboards.
- IBM Plex Sans: more humanist and engineered; good for tech/editorial crossover.
- Mona Sans: versatile variable family; good for GitHub/product-adjacent systems.
- Source Sans 3: mature and readable; quieter than trendier grotesks.
- Public Sans: civic, clear, sturdy; good for content-heavy sites.
- Rethink Sans: friendly grotesk, good for brands that want warmth.
- Onest: compact and modern; good for UI and identity work.
- Funnel Sans: slightly more characterful product sans.
- Instrument Sans: clean with design-world polish.
- Hanken Grotesk: good free substitute for many paid geometric-grotesk systems.
- Host Grotesk: useful when the target has a plain but contemporary feel.

## Geometric / Circular / Product Sans

Use when the target resembles: Futura-adjacent faces, Hamlin, Garet, Coco Gothic, Nourd, Aquawax, Ux Sans, Eudoxus Sans, Gabarito, Geologica.

- Manrope: geometric but practical; strong free replacement for product branding.
- Sora: tech-forward and slightly squared; good for crypto/web3/AI.
- Space Grotesk: geometric with visible personality; good for startup headlines.
- Outfit: clean, friendly, highly usable.
- Plus Jakarta Sans: polished product sans with warmth.
- Urbanist: more geometric and fashion-lite; good for landing pages.
- Geologica: variable, technical, broad range.
- Gabarito: chunky, approachable, modern.
- Montserrat: common but still useful for geometric brand work.
- Exo 2: more sci-fi/technical, less neutral.
- REM: contemporary and readable, good for web systems.

## Condensed / Compressed Headlines

Use when the target resembles: Obviously, Right Gothic, National 2 Compressed, Zico Sans Condensed, Selecta, Mundial Narrow, Rig Sans.

- Archivo Narrow: editorial, sturdy, practical.
- Archivo: variable width/weight family; useful when a system is needed.
- Roboto Condensed: safest UI-friendly condensed option.
- Barlow Condensed: warm, signage-like, good for posters.
- Barlow Semi Condensed: less aggressive, good for subheads.
- Oswald: punchy web headline substitute.
- League Gothic: classic tall display sans.
- Antonio: sharp and modern for headers.
- Teko: very compressed and sporty; best for display.
- Bebas Neue: ubiquitous but effective for bold headlines.
- Fjalla One: compact and sturdy.
- Pathway Extreme: broad variable range with condensed utility.

## Editorial Text Serif

Use when the target resembles: Canela, Signifier, Domaine Text, Blanco, Wulkan, Noe Text, Ogg Text, Newsserif, Nocturno, Copernicus, Vita, Francis, Pristine, Raptor Text.

- Newsreader: closest broad free choice for contemporary editorial text.
- Source Serif 4: robust, serious, excellent for long reading.
- Literata: bookish, warm, high-quality text face.
- Lora: friendly editorial serif; works well on web.
- Crimson Pro: classic book text with many weights.
- EB Garamond: historic, elegant, strong for literary projects.
- Cormorant Garamond: high-contrast and expressive; better for display/text mix.
- Spectral: readable, slightly sharp, good for editorial.
- Libre Baskerville: sturdy and traditional.
- Vollkorn: warm, durable, less fashionable.
- Libertinus Serif: academic/bookish, broad utility.
- Sentient: elegant free serif via Fontshare.
- Besley: Clarendon-ish warmth with useful display presence.
- Fanwood Text / Sorts Mill Goudy: oldstyle, literary, softer.

## Pairing Recipes

- Fashion/editorial: Instrument Serif or Bodoni Moda for display + Geist or Instrument Sans for UI.
- Literary/product: Newsreader for editorial + IBM Plex Sans for interface.
- Warm brand: Fraunces for display + Manrope or Hanken Grotesk for text.
- Tech/product: Sora or Space Grotesk for headings + Inter or Geist for UI.
- Dense poster: Archivo Narrow or Antonio for display + Source Sans 3 for supporting text.
- Code/editorial: IBM Plex Mono or Martian Mono as accent + Source Serif 4 or Newsreader for reading.
- Playful packaging: Caprasimo or Sour Gummy for display + Nunito or Grandstander for support.
- Gothic accent: UnifrakturMaguntia for wordmarks only + Source Sans 3 or Libre Baskerville for readable text.

Tufono

Mobile video calling, before video calling was everywhere

Project preview - replace with 1600 x 900 px PNG
Overview
CompanyZemoso Technologies
RoleUI Designer
CategoryCommunications / Mobile
Year2014
The Project

A mobile video calling app designed when mobile video calling was still a relatively new behavior. The scope included onboarding, call quality controls, and contact management.

What I Did

Designed the mobile workflows and interface, including onboarding, contact list, in-call screens, and call quality controls.

Challenges

Video calling on 2014-era mobile devices meant designing for unstable connections, low bandwidth, and small screens. The interface had to handle quality degradation gracefully without disrupting the call experience.

Outcome

Shipped as a mobile communication product.

Huddl.ai

A Zoom alternative with AI meeting intelligence

Project preview - replace with 1600 x 900 px PNG
Overview
CompanyInfinative Design
RoleUI/UX Designer
CategorySaaS / Collaboration
Year2021
The Project

Huddl.ai is a video meeting platform with AI-powered post-meeting summaries, action item detection, and recording analysis. It was positioned for teams that need meeting intelligence built into the meeting workflow.

What I Did

Built and maintained the design system for the app. Designed flows and visual design for in-meeting screens, post-meeting summaries, recording playback, and the dashboard, while collaborating closely with developers on handoff and design reviews.

Challenges

A meeting product competes with very strong mental models from Zoom and Teams. The design system had to support familiarity while still making Huddl's AI features feel useful and differentiated.

Outcome

Design system used across the full product lifecycle. In-meeting and post-meeting interfaces shipped to production.

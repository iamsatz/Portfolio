Side Experiment

AI Tuition for Students

A learning concept for helping students practice, ask better questions, and understand topics at their own pace.

Concept Notes

This concept looks at AI as a patient tutor, not a shortcut machine. The focus is on guidance, practice, and confidence building.

The product direction explores how students can move from confusion to clarity through smaller prompts, examples, feedback, and revision loops.

ProblemStudents often need help at the exact moment they are stuck, but tutoring support is not always available or affordable.
DirectionAn AI-assisted learning flow that helps students reason through topics instead of only giving answers.
StatusConcept exploration. Suitable for discussing AI UX, learning design, and product framing.

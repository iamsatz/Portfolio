Side Experiment

Convenience Store for India

A concept for a modern Indian convenience store experience shaped around local buying behavior, trust, and speed.

Concept Notes

This is a retail concept for an Indian convenience store that feels local, fast, and practical without becoming another generic grocery app.

The idea explores product discovery, replenishment, offers, neighborhood trust, and the small rituals that make Indian retail different from western convenience formats.

ProblemMany retail products treat Indian buying behavior as only price and delivery, missing context, trust, and local preference.
DirectionA store experience that blends quick daily needs, local relevance, and clear service design.
StatusComplete concept direction. More screens and story can be shared in conversation.

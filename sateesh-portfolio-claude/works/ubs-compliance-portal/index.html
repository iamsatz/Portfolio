UBS Compliance Portal

Designing the compliance backbone for a global bank

Project preview - replace with 1600 x 900 px PNG
Overview
CompanyAccolite Digital
RoleSr UX Designer
CategoryEnterprise / FinTech / Banking
Year2022–2023
The Project

A compliance portal that helps UBS assess the effectiveness of internal banking policies and procedures. Compliance officers use it to track audits, flag issues, and report on policy adherence across regions.

What I Did

Led a team of four designers across UI design and core flows. My role included reviewing junior designers' work, owning critical flows myself, and coordinating handoff with engineering.

Challenges

Banking compliance is dense, and every screen has regulatory implications. Translating regulatory requirements into scannable interfaces required multiple rounds of stakeholder review, while leading a team added the responsibility of keeping design quality consistent under deadline.

Outcome

Portal shipped and contributed to a 20% improvement in compliance metrics. The team delivered documented work that engineering could ship with less back-and-forth.

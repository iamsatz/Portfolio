The Project

The Pifo marketing site was designed by Arjun at HueGrid and built by me in Framer. The site shipped in 2025 and has since been archived.

What I Did

Handled the build only. Translated Arjun's static designs into a responsive, animated Framer site, including component logic, responsive breakpoints, motion, and CMS integration.

Challenges

Framer's component model has its own quirks. Translating designs faithfully while making them responsive and performant required ongoing trade-offs, and some animations had to be adapted for Framer's animation engine.

Outcome

Site shipped and was live during the Pifo launch period. Source file and screenshots from the live site are available.

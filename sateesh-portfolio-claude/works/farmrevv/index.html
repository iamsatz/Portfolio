Farmrevv

A warehouse management system for vegetable and fruit operations

Project preview - replace with 1600 x 900 px PNG
Overview
CompanyInfinative Design
RoleUI/UX Designer
CategoryAgritech / Logistics
Year2021
The Project

A mobile app for warehouse managers handling vegetable and fruit inventory. It tracks stock levels, manages orders, coordinates with suppliers, and supports the time-sensitive logistics of perishable goods.

What I Did

Designed the entire product end to end: research, flows, wireframes, visual design, and developer handoff. I was the single designer on the project.

Challenges

Perishable goods logistics is unforgiving. A wrong order, stock count, or handoff can create real food waste, so the interface needed to support high-frequency updates without overwhelming warehouse managers on the move.

Outcome

Increased operational efficiency for warehouse managers by 22%. The app shipped and was used across multiple warehouse locations.

Xperian

Internal hiring and education tools, no-code

Project preview - replace with 1600 x 900 px PNG
Overview
CompanyUX Labs
RoleUI/UX Designer
CategoryEdTech / Internal Tools
Year2019
The Project

Internal products for Xperian's design education and hiring operations, built using no-code platforms to reduce development time compared to a custom-built alternative.

What I Did

Designed the UI and flows. I also handled video and audio editing for course content, which became a cross-functional contribution beyond interface design.

Challenges

No-code platforms come with built-in constraints. Designing within those constraints required choosing patterns that worked natively instead of fighting the platform's component logic.

Outcome

Internal tools shipped and reduced development time by 30% compared with custom-built alternatives.
